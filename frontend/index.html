About This Research

This application demonstrates a custom CNN developed for three-class long bone fracture classification: Healthy, Simple Fracture, and Wedge/Complex Fracture. The model was trained using PyTorch and evaluated against pretrained CNN architectures.

Research and educational use only. This system is not validated for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions. Model confidence is not a measure of clinical certainty.
